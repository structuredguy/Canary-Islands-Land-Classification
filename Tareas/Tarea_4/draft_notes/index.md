## Executive Findings at a Glance

1. **Statutory Geometry (`Np <= 2`):** Under a strict 2-floor height cap (`S_bruta = 60,000 m2s`), `Gross FAR = 1.0` is physically unreachable without exceeding `100%` lot coverage (`111.11%` required). At a realistic `90% BCR` cap, maximum 2-floor yield sits at **`Gross FAR = 0.895`** (`53,702 m2c`, `42` plots).
2. **Economic Efficiency vs. Physical Maximum:** Because unit market prices decline as density rises and low-density luxury comps exhibit strong upside skewness, the promoter's **expected-margin peak** occurs cleanly inside the frontier around **`Gross FAR = 0.58–0.61` (`BCR ≈ 45%–49%`)**, sacrificing volume (`m2c`) to protect unit sales margins (`EUR/m2c`).
3. **Affordability Integration (`40% VPO`):** Applying the statutory `40%` VPO reserve to total buildable floor area (`m2c`, absorbing the `10%` municipal PMS cession) splits lot coverage into `(BCR_M, BCR_V)`. Even after charging **common underground garages (`+250 EUR/m2c`)** to compact VPO blocks, concentrating VPO (`90% BCR` at 2 floors or 5-floor collective towers) preserves low-density free-market villas and lifts overall optimal **`Gross FAR`** to **`0.670–0.715`**.
